## Weapon Controls & Operation

### How to Reload
1. Ensure you have **6.5×50mm 5-Round Clips** in your inventory.
2. Hold the Type 11 LMG in your main hand.
3. **Crouch + Right-Click (Sneak + Use)** to load a single 5-round clip into the side hopper.
4. Repeat up to 6 times to fill the hopper to its maximum capacity of **30 rounds**.

### How to Fire
* **Stand & Right-Click:** Unleash high-damage suppressive fire.
* Rounds fly at a significantly higher velocity and flatter trajectory than standard vanilla arrows.
